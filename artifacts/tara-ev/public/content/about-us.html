ABOUT US

We are an authorized dealership for TARA Neighborhood Electric Vehicles. This website is your gateway to purchasing TARA vehicles and getting them serviced through a trusted TARA dealership. From helping you choose the right model to keeping it running for years to come, we handle sales, service, support, and warranty care for the full TARA lineup.

Sales & Expert Guidance

As your local TARA dealership, we help you find the right vehicle for your course, community, or business — with expert guidance on the full range of T1, T2 Utility, and T3 models, plus accessories and customization options.

Help & Service

Need help with parts, maintenance, or repairs? Our dealership service team supports every TARA vehicle we sell — from routine service and diagnostics to genuine TARA parts — so you can keep your fleet on the move.

Warranty Services

As an authorized dealer, we process TARA warranty claims on your behalf and stand behind every vehicle we deliver. If something isn't right, we work directly with TARA to resolve it quickly — giving you factory-backed protection with local, personal service.
