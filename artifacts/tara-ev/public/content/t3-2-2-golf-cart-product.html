The T3 2+2 features a multi-functional dashboard, a spacious front trunk, and a built-in refrigerator. Combining luxury with practicality, it’s the perfect choice for daily excursions and adventures.

Vehicle Highlights

DASHBOARD

Enhance your riding experience with our versatile dashboard, elevating both the aesthetics and functionality. This innovative dashboard features an intuitive layout with ample storage compartments, sleek cup holders, and easy-access controls for lights and other accessories. Designed to blend style and utility seamlessly, it transforms your golf cart interior into a sophisticated and practical space.

WINDSHIELD

Featuring a convenient rotary switch, our laminated windshield allows for easy tilt angle adjustments to suit your preference. It offers exceptional durability and clarity, ensuring a clear view of the path ahead. This windshield not only provides essential protection from the elements but also enhances the overall appearance of your golf cart.

ILLUMINATED SPEAKERS

Whether you're teeing off at dawn or cruising home at dusk, Tara's Illuminated Speakers transform every ride into a sensory experience. They deliver more than just clear sound—they create an atmosphere. Deep bass, rich tones, and a stylish glow bring energy and elegance to every journey.

LED LIGHTING

Our personal transportation vehicles come standard with LED lights: high beams, low beams, daytime running lights, turn signals and brake lights for a brighter ride. Our lights are more powerful with less battery drain, delivering a 2-3 times wider field of vision compared to our competitors, allowing you to enjoy the ride worry-free, even after the sun goes down.

LUXURY SEATING

Tara's premium seats are crafted for those who value comfort as much as style. Designed with ergonomic contours that embrace your body, each seat offers exceptional support and a plush ride. Integrated armrests and headrests add to the sense of relaxation, while built-in seatbelts provide an extra layer of safety.

ELECTRIC POWER STEERING

Tara T3 Golf Carts are equipped with advanced Electric Power Steering (EPS) to deliver a smoother, more responsive driving experience. EPS reduces steering effort at low speeds and enhances stability at higher speeds, making every turn feel light, precise, and controlled. Whether you're navigating tight corners or cruising long fairways, EPS ensures confident handling with less physical effort.
