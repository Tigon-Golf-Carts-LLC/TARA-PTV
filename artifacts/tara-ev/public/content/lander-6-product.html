Vehicle Highlights

DASHBOARD

Your trusted golf cart is a reflection of who you are. Upgrades and modifications give personality and style to your vehicle. A golf cart dashboard adds beauty and functionality to your golf cart interior. The golf car accessories on dashboard are designed to improve the machine's aesthetics, comfort, and function.

OPTIONAL 7” MULTI-FUNCTIONAL TOUCHSCREEN

Touchscreen integrated with speed display, Driving Gear indication, Lights, Odometer,etc.

ACCELERATOR BRAKE PEDAL

The accelerator brake pedal provides precise control and smooth acceleration.  With its ergonomic design, it delivers comfort and reduces fatigue during long rides.​

14x 7" ALUMINUM WHEEL 215/55R12" TIRE

aluminum wheel / 225/55r 14" radial tire.Your look, your style - it starts with durable, safe golf cart wheels and tires to highlight your car. CUPHOLDER

Everyone needs a cupholder even if you're bringing a single water bottle. This cupholder in your golf cart reduces the risk of spills and makes it easier to transport soda, beer and other drinks. You can also store small accessories like USB cords in the compartments.

SEAT BACK COVER ASSEMBLY

The seat back cover assembly improves the durability and longevity of the seat backs by protecting them from damage caused by daily wear and tear. It can be easily removed and replaced, allowing for convenient cleaning and maintenance of the seat backs.
