Golf Cart GPS for Golf Courses

Course managers searching for golf cart GPS solutions are usually looking for more than just vehicle location tracking. A truly valuable system must address vehicle dispatching, turf protection, pace of play, and battery management. For clubs operating fleets of dozens or even hundreds of carts, GPS has evolved from a simple navigation tool into an integral part of golf cart fleet management.

Real-Time Tracking

With golf cart GPS, managers can monitor vehicle locations, speeds, and travel history via a backend system. Staff can quickly locate specific carts when vehicles congregate in one area or when players require assistance.

TARA Neighborhood Electric Vehicles offers two solutions—a touchscreen GPS Fleet Management System and a GPS Tracker Module—both supporting real-time tracking, allowing courses to choose the option that best fits their size and management needs.

Geofencing

Geofencing is a core feature of golf cart GPS systems. Courses can establish virtual boundaries around areas such as greens, maintenance zones, wet or soft turf, and temporarily closed sections, applying different rules based on vehicle type.

Unlike relying solely on signage, geofencing records instances of carts entering restricted areas and allows courses to adjust boundaries based on weather conditions or maintenance schedules. Effective use of this feature helps minimize the impact of carts on sensitive turf areas.

Pace of Play

Busy courses often face issues where a leading group slows down, causing a backlog of following groups. Pace-of-play monitoring tracks the progress of different groups, enabling managers to quickly identify delays at specific holes or with particular groups of players.

While GPS does not automatically resolve slow play, it provides real-time data that allows course marshals to intervene where necessary, rather than waiting for complaints from players behind the slow group.

Vehicle Status

Modern golf cart GPS systems also facilitate fleet management. The Tara system allows for monitoring of battery State of Charge (SoC), vehicle speed, fault codes, and usage history, while also supporting remote locking, scheduled locking, and basic operational analysis.

Battery data is particularly useful for electric golf cart fleets. Managers can identify vehicles with low battery levels in advance and arrange for charging or vehicle swaps, thereby reducing downtime caused by power depletion during peak hours.

Tara GPS

TARA Neighborhood Electric Vehicles positions its GPS technology as a comprehensive golf course fleet solution rather than merely a standalone tracking accessory. The touchscreen system offers features such as interactive course maps and in-vehicle messaging, while the TARA GPS Tracker Module supports cross-brand compatibility—making it ideal for golf courses that need to manage fleets comprising vehicles from different manufacturers.

Therefore, the decision to equip golf carts with GPS should be based on factors such as fleet size, course area, “pace of play” management needs, and turf protection requirements. For large golf cart fleets, the value of GPS lies primarily in operational management rather than simply allowing players to check distances.

FAQs

What does GPS do on a golf cart?

Golf cart GPS systems are used for vehicle tracking, course mapping, distance information, and fleet management. Commercial systems can also incorporate features like geofencing, pace-of-play monitoring, and vehicle status tracking.

Can you track a golf cart with GPS?

Yes. By installing a GPS system that supports real-time tracking, managers can monitor vehicle locations and usage via a backend system. Specific capabilities depend on the hardware and software configuration.

How does golf cart geofencing work?

Geofencing involves establishing virtual boundaries on a digital map. When a vehicle enters a designated zone, the system can trigger alerts, log the event, or execute pre-set vehicle management rules.

Can GPS help protect golf course turf?

It can assist with protection. Courses can use geofencing to restrict vehicle access to areas near greens, wet or soft turf, and maintenance zones, though boundary settings should be tailored to actual course conditions.

Does TARA Neighborhood Electric Vehicles offer GPS fleet management?

Yes. TARA Neighborhood Electric Vehicles offers a GPS fleet management system and GPS tracker modules that support real-time tracking, geofencing, battery monitoring, pace-of-play management, and other fleet administration functions.
