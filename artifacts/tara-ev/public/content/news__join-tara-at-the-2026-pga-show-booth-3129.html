Join Tara at the 2026 PGA Show – Booth #3129!

We are thrilled to invite you to the 2026 PGA Show, taking place January 20-23, 2026, in Orlando, Florida! As a leader in electric golf carts and advanced fleet management solutions, Tara will be showcasing our cutting-edge technologies at Booth #3129. We would love for you to visit us, explore our latest innovations, and discover how Tara can help take your golf course operations to the next level.

Whether you’re a golf course owner, operator, distributor, or an industry partner, this is an opportunity to learn firsthand how our electric golf carts can enhance the player experience, streamline fleet management, and drive new revenue opportunities for your business.

What You Can Expect at Booth #3129:

Experience the Tara Electric Golf Cart Lineup

See how Tara’s fleet of electric golf carts is designed for high performance, comfort, and efficiency. From utility vehicles to golf carts, we have a solution tailored to meet the specific needs of your course.

Explore Our GPS Fleet Management System

Learn about Tara’s GPS fleet management system, which includes real-time GPS tracking, vehicle diagnostics, and vehicle management features. Our system helps golf courses optimize operations and improve fleet performance.

Discover New Revenue Opportunities

Find out how the touchscreen of Tara’s optional GPS system can become a powerful tool for advertising, promotions, and food ordering. This integrated system helps enhance player engagement and generate more revenue for your club.

Consult With Our Experts

Our team will be on hand to provide insights, answer your questions, and discuss how Tara can provide custom solutions to fit the unique needs of your golf course. Whether you’re looking to upgrade your fleet, improve course efficiency, or expand revenue streams, we’re here to help.
