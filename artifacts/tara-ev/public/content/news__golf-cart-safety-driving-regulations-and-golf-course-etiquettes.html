Golf Cart Safety Driving Regulations and Golf Course Etiquettes

On the golf course, golf carts are not only a means of transportation, but also an extension of gentlemanly demeanor. According to statistics, 70% of accidents caused by illegal driving are caused by ignorance of basic regulations. This article systematically sorts out safety guidelines and etiquette points to help you become an elegant driver on the golf course.

Basic operating regulations: the whole process from starting to parking

1. Necessary inspections before starting

- Power and hardware detection: Before starting, you need to confirm whether the power is sufficient, and check the thickness of the brake pads and tire pressure to ensure safety.

- Bad weather: The wading depth on rainy days must not exceed 1/2 the height of the wheel hub.

2. Standardized operating procedures

- Start sequence: Start the vehicle → Confirm the gear position (forward FWD/reverse REV) → Lightly press the speed reducer with your right foot to release the brake → Gradually press the accelerator.

- Parking regulations: After parking, you need to tighten the handbrake, reset the gear to neutral, and turn off the vehicle’s main power.

Dealing with complex terrain and emergencies

Slope driving skills

- Uphill control: When the slope is greater than 15°, you should maintain a constant speed to avoid power interruption. If you slip, you should immediately reverse to flat ground and start again.
- Downhill avoidance: Use the “point brake” method (lightly press the brake every 0.5 seconds) to avoid overheating and failure caused by long-term braking.

Hidden rules of golf course etiquette

1. Driving path management

- 90-degree rule: On a wet and soft field, you need to drive along the lane to a point parallel to the ball position, turn right angles to drive into the fairway, and return to the original route after taking the ball. This can reduce the area of turf damage by 60%.
- Green restricted area: It is strictly forbidden to drive into the green area, because the repair period of the turf after being crushed by the golf cart may be as long as several months.

2. Avoidance in social scenes

- Hitting silent period: When the players in the same group are in a ready position, they need to stop and wait until the shot is completed. The minimum safe distance is 10 meters.
- Etiquette for meeting: When meeting on a narrow road, downhill vehicles should actively give way to uphill vehicles.

Maintenance responsibilities and accident handling

1. Key points for daily maintenance

- Battery maintenance: Lead-acid batteries need to be deeply discharged to 10% and then fully charged every month, and lithium batteries should avoid long-term storage at full charge (it is recommended to maintain the power at 30-80%).

2. Emergency handling of accidents

- Turf repair: If the turf is rolled, it is necessary to immediately fill it with a repair tool mixed with ryegrass seeds and nutrient soil, press it and water it for maintenance.
- Equipment repair: When a circuit failure occurs, the battery main switch must be disconnected and the maintenance personnel must be contacted. It is strictly forbidden to disassemble the controller by yourself.

Advanced skills: Improve driving economy

- Energy consumption optimization: Maintaining a constant speed of 15km/h can save 25% of electricity compared to frequent acceleration and deceleration, and try to avoid excessive load to extend battery life.
- Tire management: Measure the tread depth every month, and reduce the tire pressure to improve grip when driving on sand.

Conclusion

Safe driving regulations are the bottom line for golf cart use, and golf course etiquette embodies the essence of sportsmanship. It is recommended that players incorporate regulatory awareness into every trip. Only by paying equal attention to technology and cultivation can the true charm of golf be interpreted on the green field.
