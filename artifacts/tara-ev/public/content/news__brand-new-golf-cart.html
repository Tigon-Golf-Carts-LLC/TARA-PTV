New brand new golf carts typically utilize the latest generation of motors and control systems.

Tara’s models are equipped with high-efficiency brushless motors and intelligent controllers, enabling smoother acceleration, more powerful hill climbing, lower energy consumption, and adaptability to various terrains.

Old golf carts mostly use lead-acid batteries, while newer models generally use lithium battery systems. Tara uses self-developed high-energy-density lithium batteries, which have longer battery life, faster charging and lower maintenance costs, greatly improving the convenience of use.

3. Double improvement in safety and comfort

From hydraulic braking systems to LED smart lighting, to ergonomic seats, the new generation of Tara electric vehicles has been fully upgraded in details. Each brand new golf cart has undergone multiple safety tests to ensure stable driving.

4. Customizable design

Tara offers a variety of appearances, body colors, number of seats and accessories, allowing users to freely configure according to their use. Whether it is community patrol, vacation shuttle or high-end villa car, you can create a customized solution.

(1) Longer service life

New cars are more stable in core components such as motors, electronic control systems, and battery packs, and their overall service life is 30%-50% longer than used cars.

(2) Low failure rate

Battery failure, brake failure or wiring aging problems common in old golf carts are almost non-existent in new models. Every Tara model undergoes rigorous testing before leaving the factory to ensure stable operation.

(3) Lower maintenance costs

Brand new golf carts have longer lifespans and longer maintenance intervals after component upgrades. Taking Tara models as an example, routine maintenance costs can be reduced by more than 40% compared to traditional models.

(4) High value retention rate

High-quality new cars not only perform well in use, but also have higher resale value in the secondary market. Choosing Tara’s brand new cars means better long-term investment returns.
