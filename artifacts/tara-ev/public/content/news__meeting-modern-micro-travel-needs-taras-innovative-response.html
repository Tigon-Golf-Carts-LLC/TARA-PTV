Meeting Modern Micro-Travel Needs: Tara’s Innovative Response

In recent years, the demand for electric low speed vehicles in golf courses and some specific scenarios has been continuously upgraded: it must meet the needs of member pick-up and drop-off, as well as daily maintenance and logistics transportation; at the same time, low-carbon environmental protection and cost control have become important considerations for procurement. Tara has launched a variety of customized vehicles to help golf courses operate efficiently, optimize vehicle management, and lead the trend of green and low-carbon operations.

New Characteristics of Demand

1. Diversified Participating Groups

With the popularization of golf, more and more novices and family users have joined, and golf courses need to flexibly dispatch vehicles between member pick-up and drop-off, safety inspections and daily maintenance.

2. Comprehensive Use of Multiple Scenarios

In addition to traditional golf course transportation, it also includes short-distance shuttles and material distribution in resorts, communities, and parks. Vehicles need to take into account both passenger and cargo functions.

3. Green and Sustainable Demands

Energy conservation and emission reduction, noise control and full life cycle costs have become the focus of the industry. Small electric low speed vehicles are favored due to their zero emissions and low maintenance characteristics.

Application Expansion of Low Speed Vehicles

1. Resorts and Communities

In high-end resorts and closed communities, low speed vehicles are used for tourist pick-up, facility inspections and catering delivery, providing a one-car-multi-purpose solution for various scenarios.

2. Parks and Campuses

In large parks and schools, electric low speed vehicles replace traditional tractors and fuel vehicles for campus patrols, maintenance tool transportation and emergency rescue. They are favored by management for their low noise and no exhaust emissions.

3. Commercial and Public Spaces

In public places such as commercial complexes and parks, such vehicles can be used for security inspections, cleaning and event support. They are flexible and cost-effective, effectively improving overall operational efficiency.

Tara’s Customized Solutions

Tara provides a diverse product line covering multiple scenarios, including golf carts designed for players, utility vehicles that meet the maintenance and logistics needs of the golf course, and a personal series of electric vehicles for short and medium-distance travel, jointly building a one-stop micro-travel ecosystem.

1. Golf Cart

Tara golf carts include a variety of models with different functions and configurations, such as Harmony and Spirit, to meet the needs of transporting golf courses and events of different levels.

2. Utility Vehicles

To cope with golf course maintenance, logistics transportation and rapid material allocation, Tara launched the utility vehicle Turfman, equipped with a large-capacity cargo box, which can flexibly perform tasks such as lawn maintenance, equipment handling and garbage removal, effectively simplifying the dispatch process and reducing operating costs.

3. Personal Series

Tara’s Personal Series electric vehicles are designed for short- and medium-distance travel in communities, resorts and private estates. They combine stylish appearance and excellent battery life, provide comfortable seats, floor pedals and portable storage space, and take into account both leisure and practicality.

4. Flexible Customized Configuration

For different usage scenarios, Tara provides a wealth of optional packages and customized services, including a variety of body colors, seat styles, tire sizes, and accessories such as multimedia audio and entertainment systems, so that customers can create the most suitable exclusive models according to their budget and needs.

Through a rich product line and flexible customization options, Tara helps customers achieve dual improvements in efficiency and experience in multiple fields such as golf course operations, park management, and personal travel.

Future Outlook

As “safety, flexibility, and low carbon” become the new normal in the industry, the demand for electric low speed vehicles in golf courses and surrounding scenes will further deepen. Tara will continue to invest in battery technology and vehicle design innovation, continuously enrich customized optional solutions, and work with partners and customers to jointly promote golf and related fields into a new era of efficiency and greenness.
