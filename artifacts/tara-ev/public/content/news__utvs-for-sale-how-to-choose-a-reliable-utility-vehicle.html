I. Definition and Application of UTVs

What does a UTV stand for?

UTV, short for “Utility Task Vehicle,” is also known as a side-by-side vehicle, deriving its name from its two-person side-by-side seating design. UTVs are characterized by their strong off-road performance and load-carrying capacity, making them suitable for a variety of scenarios:

Farms and ranches: transporting supplies, patrolling, or towing equipment.

Outdoor activities: off-road adventures, hunting, and camping.

Commercial use: commuting to tourist attractions, resort transportation, and community patrols.

Personal leisure: family entertainment and rural travel.

Compared to ATVs (All Terrain Vehicles), UTVs emphasize multi-person driving and cargo capacity, making them more similar to small trucks in terms of functionality.
