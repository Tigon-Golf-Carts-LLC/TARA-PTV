Street-legal golf carts: Everything you need to know

Choosing a street-legal golf cart means you have more freedom. But it also requires understanding the relevant regulations, modification requirements, and high-quality models, such as the T2 Turfman 700 EEC launched by Tara, which is currently certified for street use.

1. What kind of golf cart is street-legal?

In order for a golf cart to be street-legal, it must meet local regulations for low-speed vehicles (NEV or LSV). Common requirements include:

Lighting system: headlights, taillights, turn signals

Rearview mirrors (left and right and inside the car) and brake lights

Front windshield that complies with traffic regulations

All seats must be equipped with seat belts

Horn, parking brake

Top speed is usually limited to 25 miles (about 40 kilometers)

For example, Tara’s T2 Turfman 700 EEC is a model with an EEC certificate of compliance, which can meet road driving requirements in areas that allow low-speed vehicles.

2. Can golf carts be driven on public roads?

The answer is yes, but only if it is allowed in your area. For example, in many states in the United States, roads with speed limits under 35 miles per hour allow legal street-type golf carts to pass. But pay special attention to the following matters:

Local traffic laws on NEVs

Whether registration, insurance or driver’s license is required

Are there any restrictions on the route or special permits required

In essence, a legal golf cart has changed from a “field transportation tool” to a true “road vehicle”.

3. How to convert an ordinary golf cart to a road-legal one?

The following modifications need to be installed:

Full set of lighting system (headlights, brake lights, turn signals)

Rearview mirrors (left and right + interior)

Seat belts for all seats

DOT-certified windshield

Horn and reflective stickers

Ensure the brake system is compliant

Adjust the speed limit to less than 25 miles per hour

However, personal modification is difficult and may affect the original factory warranty. Therefore, it is more worry-free and safe to choose a model such as Tara T2 Turfman 700 EEC that is compliant from the factory.

How to choose a suitable street legal golf cart?

Understand local regulations: Are NEV/LSVs allowed to drive? Do you need to register?

Determine the power type: electric is more environmentally friendly and quiet; fuel is suitable for long-distance use
