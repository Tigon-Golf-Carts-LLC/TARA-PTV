Vehicle Highlights

RETRACTABLE RUNNING BOARD

Heavy duty retractable running board makes your car look off-road ready and make getting in and out of your golf cart easier, while also protecting the side frames and body of your golf cart. It can also be folded up to reduce the size when necessary.

TILTABLE LAMINATED WINDSHIELD

The innovative rotary switch windshield offers effortless adjustment with a simple turn. Whether you want to block out the wind or enjoy a refreshing breeze, the choice is yours, offering a customizable driving experience tailored to your preference.

HYDRAULIC DISC BRAKE

Using Four-Wheel Hydraulic Piston Disc Brake. They have lighter weight and easier to maintain. Stronger braking capacity means the vehicle has a shorter braking distance to protect the safety of passengers.

LED LIGHTING

Illuminate the night with unparalleled brilliance. These high-performance LED lights provide exceptional brightness, ensuring a clear view during nighttime driving. Meticulously engineered for durability and longevity, they consume significantly less power compared to traditional lighting, making them a cost-effective choice in the long run.

FRONT TRUNK

Ample storage space ensures that you can easily carry everything you need whether you are on the golf course or outdoors. It is designed to offer unparalleled storage solutions without compromising style or functionality.

OPTIONAL REFRIGERATOR

The optional built-in removable refrigerator provides ease of use and flexibility, making it an ideal solution for keeping food and drinks cool on the go. This compact yet spacious refrigerator seamlessly integrates the golf cart, offering ample storage space without sacrificing style or functionality.

REDEFINING OUTDOOR EXCELLENCE

With the T3 2+2 Lifted, your adventures are lifted to new heights. The silent off-road tires provide a smooth and quiet ride, allowing you to explore uncharted territories with ease. Enjoy a journey that is both serene and exhilarating, as this vehicle effortlessly combines comfort and excitement.
