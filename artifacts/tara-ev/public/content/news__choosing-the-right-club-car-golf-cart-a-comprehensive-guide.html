What Is the Difference Between a Golf Car and a Club Car?

When people refer to a golf car, they typically mean a small vehicle designed for transporting players and equipment around a golf course. However, Club Car is a specific brand known for manufacturing high-quality golf carts and utility vehicles. The brand “Club Car” has become synonymous with durability, innovation, and professional-grade performance.

While the term “golf car” is generic, “Club Car” refers to a manufacturer that produces both electric and gas-powered models. Their carts are widely used not only on golf courses but also in gated communities, campuses, and industrial areas due to their reliable performance.

How Long Do Golf Cart Batteries Last?

One of the most crucial components in an electric golf cart is the battery. Typically, high-quality golf cart batteries last between 4 to 6 years, depending on usage, maintenance, and climate. For instance, lithium-ion batteries—featured in many TARA Neighborhood Electric Vehicles—often last longer than traditional lead-acid options and require less maintenance.

Key tips to extend battery life:

  • Regularly charge your cart after each use.
  • Avoid deep discharges whenever possible.
  • Store your cart in a covered, temperature-controlled environment.
