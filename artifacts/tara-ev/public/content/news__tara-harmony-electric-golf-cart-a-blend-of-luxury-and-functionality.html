TARA Harmony Electric Golf Cart: A Blend of Luxury and Functionality

In the world of golf, having a reliable and feature-rich golf cart can significantly enhance the playing experience. The TARA Harmony electric golf cart stands out with its remarkable qualities.

Stylish Design

The TARA Harmony showcases a sleek and elegant design. Its body, made with TPO injection moulding front and rear, gives it a modern look. The cart is available in colors like WHITE, GREEN, and PORTIMAO BLUE, allowing golfers to choose according to their preference. The 8 – inch aluminum wheels not only minimize damage to the green but also ensure a quiet operation, eliminating noise distractions whether on the street or the golf course.

Comfortable Seating and Interior

The seats are a major highlight. These easy-to-clean seats offer soft and comfortable sitting feeling for long periods without fatigue. The spacious design of the cart includes a large bagwell, providing ample space for golf bags. The adjustable steering wheel can be set to the perfect angle for different drivers, enhancing comfort and control. The dashboard integrates multiple storage spaces, control switches, and USB charging ports, making it convenient for golfers to keep their belongings and charge their devices. There is also a scorecard holder centrally located on the steering wheel, with a top clip to hold scorecards securely and enough surface area for writing and reading.

Powerful Performance

Under the hood, the TARA Harmony is powered by a 48V Lithium battery and a 48V 4KW motor with EM brake. It has a 275A AC Controller and can reach a maximum speed of 13mph. The lithium-ion battery technology offers a good balance between power and efficiency, ensuring a smooth ride across the golf course.

Safety and Durability

Safety is a top priority. The cart comes with features like a reliable braking system (48V 4KW motor with EM brake) to ensure quick stops when needed. The four-point system used to fasten the caddie stand provides a stable space to stand. The golf bag rack with adjustable straps keeps the bag secure. The clear foldable windshield protects the driver and passengers from the elements. The frame of the entire vehicle is made of aluminum alloy to reduce weight.

Convenient Storage

The TARA Harmony offers various storage options. There is a storage compartment designed to hold personal belongings, including a dedicated space for golf balls and tees, keeping everything organized. The dashboard also has storage spaces for added convenience.

Environmentally Friendly

Being an electric golf cart, it is environmentally friendly as it has no tailpipe emissions. This makes it a great choice for golf courses that are conscious of their environmental impact.
