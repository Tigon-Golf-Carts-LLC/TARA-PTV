Tara Spirit Pro blends luxury and innovation in a sleek electric golf cart tailored for golf course. Its energy-efficient lithium battery, ergonomic features, and stylish design ensure smooth, quiet rides across the greens. With ample storage and durable 8-inch wheels, this golf course fleet vehicle offers both performance and lasting appeal — ideal for modern course operations.

Vehicle Highlights

LUXURY SEATS

The newly designed, luxury seats offer a superb riding experience. Their seamless surface design ensures convenient daily cleaning and maintenance, while the durable construction withstands various weather conditions and provide consistent support. Additionally, the seats come with safety handrails to ensure your safety while riding.

COMFORT GRIP STEERING WHEEL

The steering wheel featuring a comfortable grip and responsive handling with scorecard holder. Even your pencil has its place. Its adjustable steering wheel is meticulously designed to enhance driving ease and provide the driver with optimal control over their driving view and the distance to the wheel. The ergonomic design ensures a comfortable grip, giving you precise control over every maneuver.

STORAGE COMPARTMENT

The overhead storage compartment makes it easy to store your gloves, caps, towels, and more things. The clever design makes it integrated into the roof. Just reach out and get whatever you want.

HEAVY-DUTY CANOPY

The heavy-duty injection-molded canopy is connected to the vehicle body through aluminum supports, which provide protection from sun and rain. It also integrates features such as handles and a storage compartment.

MODERNIZED FRONT END & DASH

Our custom-designed and manufactured front cover boasts an impressive, unique, and futuristic appearance. The front cover and lampshade are easy to disassemble, and the internal wiring is reserved, which can be easily customized and equipped with headlights.

8" LAWN TIRES

Navigate the greens effortlessly with our specially-designed tires. Fitted with 8-inch rims, they’re not just about good looks.  Designed thoughtfully, their flat tread ensures the greens remain undamaged. Experience a seamless ride, regardless of the terrain.
