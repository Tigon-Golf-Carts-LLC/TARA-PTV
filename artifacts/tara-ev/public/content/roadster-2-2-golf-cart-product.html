Tara Roadster 2+2 enhances both your golf game and neighborhood cruise. This golf and leisure cart comes standard with premium features — like luxury seats — designed to help you relax and enjoy every ride. Whether you’re navigating the course or coasting through the community, its neighborhood-friendly design delivers comfort, style, and versatility.

Vehicle Highlights

TARA LUXURY SEAT

TARA’s luxury seats are incredibly well-rounded. Whether you’re looking for comfort, protection, aesthetics, or all three, our seat designs have you covered. Our luxury seats include soft-touch imitation leather, well carved with an Exotic pattern . Get yourself comfortable while you are going on cruising for personal transits.

SMART TOUCHSCREEN

CarPlay in Tara can effortlessly connect your iPhone to the cart, accessing essential apps like phone, navigation, and music through the onboard display. Whether cruising the golf course or driving leisurely, CarPlay provides an intuitive and convenient interface, keeping you focused on the road or course. Additionally, it supports Android Auto, ensuring that Android users enjoy the same seamless smart connectivity.

MULTI-FUNCTIONAL DASHBOARD

Your trusted golf cart is a reflection of who you are. Upgrades and modifications give personality and style to your vehicle. A golf cart dashboard adds beauty and functionality to your golf cart interior. The golf car accessories on dashboard are designed to improve the machine's aesthetics, comfort, and function.

CUBOID SOUND BAR

The cuboid sound bar is a sleek and innovative addition that provides high-quality audio entertainment on the go. Easily controlled via a multi-function touch screen, it allows you to stream  favorite music effortlessly. The ring-shaped lights can sync with the rhythm to create a dynamic and engaging atmosphere.

FLIP-FLOP REAR SEAT & STORAGE KIT

The rear seats offer the same luxurious comfort as the front seats, featuring curved armrests that provide a better fit for your arms. A hidden storage space beneath the seat allows you to store belongings securely. Additionally, the rear handrail and the footrest are equipped, enhancing the functionality and convenience.

12" ALUMINUM WHEEL WITH RADIAL TIRE

This 12" alloy tire boasts an advanced flat tread design that optimizes water dispersion, significantly enhancing performance. This reduces the risk of riding and improves overall driving stability, ensuring a safer and more controlled driving experience.
