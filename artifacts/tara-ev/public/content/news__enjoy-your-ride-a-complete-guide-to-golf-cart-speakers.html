Enjoy Your Ride: A Complete Guide to Golf Cart Speakers

Want to enhance your ride with crystal-clear sound? Whether you’re cruising the course or driving through a private estate, golf cart speakers can completely transform your driving experience.

What Are Golf Cart Speakers Used For?

Golf cart speakers bring entertainment and functionality to your electric cart. From playing music via Bluetooth to receiving GPS directions or listening to your favorite podcast, speakers make the ride more enjoyable for both drivers and passengers.

Modern speakers on golf carts are wireless, weather-resistant, and designed to be compatible with electric systems, making them ideal for both personal and professional use.

Are Bluetooth Speakers Good for Golf Carts?

Absolutely. Bluetooth speakers for golf carts are now one of the most popular add-ons. They’re easy to install, portable or integrated, and connect seamlessly to smartphones or onboard infotainment systems.

Benefits of Bluetooth speakers include:

  • Wireless connectivity (no messy cables)
  • Compact size with high output
  • Rechargeable batteries or integration with golf cart power
  • Water-resistant and dustproof design

If you want factory-installed solutions, many Tara models include speaker options. For example, the Spirit Plus can be equipped with integrated sound systems that blend audio performance and style.

What Types of Golf Cart Speakers Are Available?

There are three main categories:

  1. Portable Bluetooth Speakers – These clip on easily and can be removed after your ride. Great for users who prefer flexibility.
  2. Mounted Marine-Grade Speakers – These are installed on roofs, under seats, or on dashboard panels. They are waterproof and ideal for carts used in wet conditions.
  3. Built-in Audio Systems – Offered by manufacturers like Tara, these systems come with touchscreen controls, radio, USB input, and sometimes subwoofers.

Want to customize your audio setup? Many carts from the T1 Golf Cart Series can be upgraded with high-end speaker units or multi-zone sound systems.

Where Do You Mount Speakers on a Golf Cart?

Speakers on golf carts can be mounted in several locations:

  • Under the dash or inside dashboard panels
  • On the top roof bar or canopy support
  • Inside the rear body panel or seat backs

Choose your mounting location based on sound projection, available space, and wiring access. Weather-resistant wiring and brackets are important for long-term durability.

Some premium models, such as the Explorer 2+2, are designed to accommodate factory speaker placement, making installation seamless.

Can I Install Speakers on My Existing Golf Cart?

Yes, retrofitting speakers to an existing cart is very common. You’ll need:

  • A 12V power source or converter if your cart is 48V
  • Mounting brackets or enclosures
  • Weatherproof speaker components
  • Optional amplifier for better sound output

Professional installation is recommended for built-in systems. But for plug-and-play Bluetooth units, many users opt for DIY setups.

If you’re unsure where to start, explore Tara’s line of golf cart accessories to find compatible speaker kits, mounts, and customization options.

What Should I Consider When Buying Golf Cart Speakers?

Key factors to look for include:

  • Sound Quality: Crisp audio and enough volume to be heard over wind
  • Durability: Waterproof, dustproof, and UV-resistant materials
  • Power Compatibility: Matches your cart’s battery system (12V/48V)
  • Mounting Options: Flexible positioning and easy access to controls
  • Integration: With GPS, phone, or infotainment if needed

Look for speakers that enhance both style and function without draining the battery excessively. Lithium-powered carts like Tara’s ensure consistent voltage, which supports stable audio output.

Golf cart speakers are more than just an audio upgrade—they improve the overall driving experience. Whether you prefer built-in systems, clip-on Bluetooth speakers, or fully integrated sound packages, there’s a perfect fit for every style of golf cart and every kind of user.
