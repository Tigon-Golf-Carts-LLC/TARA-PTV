The Turfman 700 is built to handle heavy-duty tasks with ease and efficiency. With its spacious cargo bed, you can easily transport heavier materials or equipment. With its sturdy tow hooks and heavy-duty front bumper, it’s capable of getting the job done in more situations. Whether it’s hauling materials on the course or towing equipment across the field, the Turfman 700 is reliable.

Vehicle Highlights

FRONT BUMPER

The heavy-duty front bumper protects the vehicle from minor impacts and scratches, allowing you to work with less worry and maximizing the service life of the vehicle.

CUP HOLDER

Want a drink while driving or working? No problem. The cup holders are just a finger's reach away and you'll find what you need.

LIFTABLE CARGO BOX

The cargo box makes it easy to transport a variety of tools and materials, whether on the golf course, farm or other venues. The lift design makes unloading easy, and the optional electric lift bar further enhances convenience.

TOWING HOOK

The towing hook combines superior strength and durability for towing a wide range of lawn equipment as well as light vehicles. Say goodbye to outside towing services and get towing jobs done quickly, saving valuable time and money.

OFF-ROAD THREAD TIRE

Quiet tire with off-road thread can better adapt to various complex terrains. It is easy to handle both grass and dirt roads.

USB CHARGING PORT

The USB charging port provides sufficient energy for your mobile devices, so you can say goodbye to battery anxiety.

Specifications

Excellent Terrain Adaptability

The Turfman 700 combines smart engineering with powerful power, and its dedicated off-road tires make it easy to navigate the golf course, muddy fields, gravel trails or mountain roads. It maintains a precise and stable ride to ensure your cargo arrives safely. The Turfman 700 is an indispensable partner for all commercial operations.
