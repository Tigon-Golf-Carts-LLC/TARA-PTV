Frequently Asked Questions

What credit score do I need to finance a golf cart?

With six lending partners we cover every credit profile. Scores of 650+ typically qualify for 0% APR promotions; 580+ for standard installment loans; and rent-to-own or in-house programs accept all credit types.

How does in-house financing work?

We finance you directly — no bank required. Approval is based on your income rather than your credit score, and terms are customized to your situation.

Is no-down-payment financing available?

Yes. Qualified buyers may be eligible for $0 down programs across good, fair, and challenged credit tiers. Call us to see if you qualify — no hard credit pull needed.

What are typical loan terms and monthly payments?

Terms range from 12 to 72 months. Use the payment calculator above to estimate your monthly payment at any price, rate, and term.

How quickly can I get approved?

Most lending partners provide decisions within minutes, and same-day approval is common. You can often complete the whole process — application to signed agreement — in one day.

Can I refinance my existing golf cart loan?

In many cases, yes. Call our financing team at 844-844-3432 and we’ll review your current loan and available refinancing options.
