Tara Roadster 2+2: Bridging the Gap Between Golf Carts and Urban Mobility

In response to the growing demand for versatile and eco-friendly transportation options, TARA Neighborhood Electric Vehicles is thrilled to announce the Roadster 2+2, offering a sustainable and efficient solution for short-distance travel in urban and suburban areas.


The Tara Roadster 2+2 combines the best of golf cart design with advanced automotive technology, making the vehicle ideal for a range of uses, from neighborhood commutes to campus transportation. Built with safety in mind, the Roadster model is featuring essential safety components like seat belts, mirrors, and lighting systems. With a top speed of 25 mph, Tara Roadster 2+2 is perfect for navigating low-speed roads and residential areas.

Each Tara Roadster 2+2 is powered by a high-efficiency lithium battery, ensuring zero emissions and low operating costs. The vehicle is equipped with spacious interiors, ergonomic seating, and advanced multimedia system, making them as comfortable as they are practical. Whether used for leisure, work, or daily commutes, Roadster provide a versatile and green transportation solution.

The radial tire design in Tara Roadster 2+2 enhances the overall driving experience by ensuring a more even distribution of pressure across the tire’s footprint, reducing wear and extending tire life. Additionally, the larger 12-inch size contributes to a more comfortable ride by absorbing road imperfections and minimizing vibrations.

The combination of these advanced tires with the vehicle’s precision suspension system ensures that every trip in a Roadster is as enjoyable as it is efficient, offering both comfort and reliability regardless of transporting passengers around a resort, commuting through a neighborhood, or running errands in the city.

As urban areas continue to embrace low-speed vehicles for their environmental benefits and convenience, TARA Neighborhood Electric Vehicles is poised to lead the market with its innovative personal LSV series, setting new standards for quality and performance in this emerging segment.
