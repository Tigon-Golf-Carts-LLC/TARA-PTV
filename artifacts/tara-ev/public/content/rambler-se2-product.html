Tara RAMBLER SE2 is a fusion of luxury and efficiency, featuring all-climate leather seats and a durable injection mould canopy. Its spacious design includes a large bagwell and energy-efficient LED lighting, complemented by stylish 10-inch aluminum wheels. An adjustable steering wheel ensures tailored driving comfort and control on the greens.

Vehicle Highlights

SEAT & ALUMINUM CANOPY

Tell those old and boring seats goodbye and say hello to the new and improved TARA Seats! These seats are made of breathable foam padding, soft and doubly long sitting without fatigue, adding better comfort to your ride.

INJECTION MOULD CANOPY

This new Injection Mould Canopy is designed for maximum strength and durability with a lower profile to provide additional overhead clearance.

GOLF CART BAG RACK

A golf cart bag rack keeps your bag secure with straps that can be adjusted and tightened making your clubs easily accessible.

SCORECARD HOLDER

Centrally located on the steering wheel, this holder features a top clip to securely hold most golf scorecards. Its expansive surface ensures ample room for both writing and reading.

8‘’ ALUMINUM WHEEL

Say goodbye to noise distractions! Whether you're driving on the street or on the golf course, the quiet operation of our tires ensures that you'll enjoy a peaceful driving experience.

CADDIE STAND

Elegantly crafted in deluxe black, this caddie stand boasts high-grade, fade-resistant cushions that resist mold. Securely fastened with a four-point system, it offers a broad and stable space to stand.
