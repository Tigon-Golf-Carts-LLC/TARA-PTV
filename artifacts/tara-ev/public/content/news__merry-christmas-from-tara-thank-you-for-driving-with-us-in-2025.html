Merry Christmas from Tara – Thank You for Driving with Us in 2025

As 2025 draws to a close, the Tara team extends its sincerest Christmas greetings to our customers, partners, and all our friends across the United States who support us.

This year has been one of rapid growth for Tara across the country. We not only delivered golf carts to more courses, but also continuously improved our services and product experience, allowing more and more course managers and members to experience Tara’s professionalism and reliability.

Tara Continues to Steadily Grow Across the United States in 2025

1. The Sun Belt: Rapid Growth, High Customer Satisfaction

In warm-weather markets across the American Sun Belt, Tara delivered its fleet to multiple golf courses through local authorized dealers. The stability, power output, and range of the vehicles were highly praised by course managers.

The number of courses using Tara fleets is growing rapidly.

Customer feedback shows a significant increase in member satisfaction.

The application of the intelligent management system helps courses optimize fleet scheduling.

2. The South and Southwest: Reliable Performance

Desert and high-heat regions place higher requirements on the heat resistance and stability of golf carts. Tara golf carts, with their advanced design and high-performance lithium batteries, have been successfully delivered to golf courses in warm-weather regions across the American South and Southwest.

Deliveries completed at multiple high-end golf courses.

Highly praised by customers, becoming a reliable golf cart partner in the region.

3. Environmentally Conscious Courses: A Green and Intelligent Choice

American golf courses are increasingly focused on environmental protection and energy efficiency. Tara’s lithium-ion battery-powered golf carts stand out in terms of low energy consumption, zero emissions, and quiet operation.

Tara golf cart fleets have been successfully deployed at courses across the country.

Improved golf course operating efficiency and reduced maintenance costs.

4. Coast to Coast: Expanding Influence and Creating a High-Quality Experience

From coast to coast, Tara further expanded its presence, entering more golf courses through local dealers and partners.

Providing golf courses with complete solutions from fleet deployment to after-sales training

Customers gave positive feedback on vehicle comfort, power stability, and after-sales responsiveness.

Highlights and Achievements of 2025

This year, Tara’s growth was not only reflected in quantity but also in quality and service:

Record-breaking fleet deliveries: Thousands of golf carts were delivered to golf courses across the United States throughout the year.

Positive market feedback: Customer satisfaction continued to improve.

Advanced intelligent management system adoption: More and more golf courses adopted Tara’s fleet dispatch and monitoring system.

Optimized after-sales service: Ensuring timely responses for customers.

Enhanced brand influence: In the American golf community, Tara has become synonymous with high quality, reliability, and innovation.

With 2026 approaching, Tara will continue to focus on customer needs, driving product, technology, and service upgrades:

1. Technological Innovation

Launch more high-performance lithium-ion battery-powered golf carts

Continuously optimize safety and comfort to provide a better experience for golf course members.

2. Nationwide Growth

Continuing to expand our reach across the United States

Deepening our partnerships with more high-end golf courses and clubs to achieve localized operational services

Bringing Tara’s high-quality fleet to more course managers and members
