---
name: Overseas text audit policy
description: US-only framing rule for TARA news content and how to audit it after merges
---

Rule: TARA is presented as a US-only dealership. No news article body, listing-card excerpt, or image alt text may claim overseas or "global/worldwide/export" deliveries, installations, service networks, or customers as TARA's own work, and no article may be framed for a foreign market (UK/EU/Asia buyer guides, non-English pieces).

**Why:** Client wants a fully US-based dealership identity; reviewers reject even generic "global customers/export experience" phrasing, not just named foreign places.

**How to apply:** After any merge, audit article bodies AND listing-card excerpts (they duplicate intros) AND alt text for foreign place names plus global/worldwide/overseas/international/export wording; deleted overseas articles have been restored by merges before, so re-check they stay gone.
