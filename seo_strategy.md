# SEO Strategy — TARA Electric Vehicles

## Site overview
Marketing and product site for TARA electric golf carts and utility vehicles — a clone/migration of taragolfcart.com. Serves as an authorized dealership site (not the manufacturer site directly).

## In scope
- All public-facing routes: home, vehicle series pages (T1/T2/T3), product pages, accessories, fleet, support/warranty/safety, about, contact, blog/news articles

## Out of scope
- No authenticated / dashboard pages exist; entire site is public

## Target audience
- Golf course operators and fleet managers (primary)
- Resort, campus, and community transportation buyers
- Individual NEV / street-legal cart buyers

## Primary keywords
- Electric golf carts, golf course carts, fleet golf carts
- TARA vehicle model names (Harmony, Spirit Pro, Spirit Plus, Roadster 2+2, Explorer 2+2, Turfman series, T3 series)
- Neighborhood electric vehicles (NEV), utility vehicles

## Rendering mode
- React + Vite SPA served via Replit static hosting
- No SSR; all per-page metadata and content rendered client-side
- Public static files under `artifacts/tara-ev/public/` are served directly
