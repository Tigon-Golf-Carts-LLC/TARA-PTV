# Turfman 700 EEC

**Page title:** Turfman 700 EEC – Street-Legal Electric Utility Vehicle

**Category:** Electric Utility Vehicle

## Description

Turfman 700 with EEC certification. This vehicle meets the EU regulations on safety, environmental protection, noise and other aspects, and can be legally driven on the road in Europe. The Turfman 700 EEC is equipped with a large-capacity cargo box to meet the needs of various working conditions such as golf course maintenance, green maintenance and logistics supply.
