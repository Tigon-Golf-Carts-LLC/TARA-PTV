Why Finance Your Golf Cart with TIGON?
Buying a golf cart outright isn't always the right move — and it doesn't have to be. TIGON Golf Carts works with a network of six national lenders so we can offer competitive financing for almost every buyer, regardless of credit history. Whether you're buying a $5,000 pre-owned cart or a $20,000 street-legal LSV, our finance team will walk you through every option and help you choose the terms that work for your budget.

Our 0% APR promotional financing (on qualifying new cart purchases) means you pay exactly the sticker price — spread over 12 to 60 months with no interest added. For buyers with fair or challenged credit, our rent-to-own and in-house programs ensure that a credit score never stands between you and your cart.
